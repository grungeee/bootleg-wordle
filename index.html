01 / THE FIRST CHAPTER

001

I liked a game.
So I built my own.

The word game was the first project I programmed entirely myself. The visual design was mine too.

I enjoyed playing Wordle and wanted to make my own version. It became a way to learn programming by making something I actually wanted to use.

The lavender background, chunky tiles and bright colours are part of that original identity. This is more than a demo to me. It is where I started.
