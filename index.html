01 / THE BUILD

A word game in one static folder.

This page is hand written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no bundler, no tracking, no accounts. Open it, play it, share the grid. It is the smallest thing I could build that still feels finished.

Scoring

Repeated letters are where word games break. The check runs twice: exact matches first, then everything else, so a green tile can never be stolen by an earlier yellow one.

State

Today's word is a hash of the date, so every visitor gets the same puzzle without a server. Progress, streaks and the guess distribution stay in local storage on your own device.

Interface

Playable from a physical keyboard or the on screen one. Native dialogs handle focus and escape, motion respects your system setting, and every control is a real button.

KNOWN LIMITS

Stats live in your browser, so they do not follow you between devices. The answer pool is filtered down to common words: a puzzle should not be lost to an obscure one.

How to play

Guess the word in 6 tries.

Each guess must be a real 5 letter word. Hit enter to submit.

After each guess the tiles change colour to show how close you were.
